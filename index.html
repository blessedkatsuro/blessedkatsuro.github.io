About me

The person
behind the
person.

I'm Blessed, an executive support and brand operations professional who helps leaders turn busy, scattered days into clear priorities and finished work.

My career started on the front line in sales and customer service, then grew into eight years as an Executive Assistant to directors in a Johannesburg manufacturing business, where I ran office operations, managed accounts and built the systems that kept the company organised.

From there I moved into remote work, supporting high-profile US clients as an Executive Virtual Assistant, and then into the office of the CEO at Zanga African Metrics, a leadership-assessment and human-capital company. As the CEO's right hand I managed the calendar, inbox, briefs and tender preparation, and took on more and more of the brand and marketing work, which led to my promotion to Brand & Marketing Lead.

Today I combine both sides: the operational discipline of a Chief of Staff and the creative, commercial eye of a brand lead. I bring steadiness, discretion and follow-through, and I care about making the leaders I work with look and feel in control.
